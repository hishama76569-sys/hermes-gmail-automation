Hermes Gmail Automation connects Hisham's Gmail account to a personal Windows installation of Hermes to read, organize, and act on email through owner-authorized workflows.

What the integration can do

01 / READ

Find and process email

Read messages and relevant message data for tasks such as searching, summarizing, and preparing replies.

02 / ORGANIZE

Update the mailbox

Apply labels and change message state, including marking messages read, archiving, or moving them to Trash.

03 / ACT

Draft and send

Compose email and send messages only through workflows and to recipients authorized by the owner.

The requested Gmail permission is gmail.modify. It includes read, compose, send, and mailbox modification access; it does not permit immediate permanent deletion that bypasses Trash.

A local workflow with clear data boundaries

OAuth credentials are stored in protected local Hermes files. Email content may be retained in local conversations, logs, or workflow outputs. Configured external integrations may receive relevant data when the owner instructs them to.

This is a personal integration, not a hosted public service. This static website cannot read Gmail, run workflows, or accept credentials. The privacy policy explains processing, retention, sharing, and revoking access.
