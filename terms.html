Terms of use

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Hermes Gmail Automation is a personal Windows integration maintained by Hisham. These terms describe its intended use. This website provides information only; it does not offer a hosted email service, public accounts, or access to anyone's mailbox.

Authorized account and workflows

The integration is intended for the owner's personal Gmail workflows. Use it only with an account the owner is authorized to access and manage. The owner is responsible for defining permitted workflows and recipients, granting Google OAuth permissions, and reviewing the actions configured in Hermes.

Email may be sent only through owner-authorized workflows and to authorized recipients. Mailbox changes, including moving messages to Trash, must stay within the owner's instructions.

Review consequential actions

Model-generated summaries, drafts, and workflow decisions can be incomplete or incorrect. Review outputs and consequential email or mailbox actions before relying on them. The owner remains responsible for the content, recipients, and effects of authorized workflows.

The integration depends on the local computer, Hermes, the configured model, and Google's APIs. Availability and behavior may change; no particular uptime or error-free result is promised.

Data and external services

The privacy policy explains Gmail access, local processing and retention, external integrations, and deletion. The owner is responsible for protecting the Windows installation and local credentials, managing retained data, and choosing which external services may receive data.

Use of Gmail, Google OAuth, and any configured external service remains subject to that service's applicable terms and policies. Use the integration in accordance with applicable law and the rights of message senders and recipients.

Stopping use

The owner may stop the integration at any time by stopping workflows and revoking its access in Google Account permissions. Local credentials, conversations, logs, and workflow outputs can be deleted separately. Revoking access does not undo messages already sent or mailbox changes already completed.
